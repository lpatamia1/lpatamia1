About Us

Welcome to Kelp Wanted, a small, sea-inspired haven created to capture Seabrook’s calm spirit. Our home features a soothing green interior, cozy nooks, and everything you need for a peaceful coastal stay.

We’re a small family who feels most at peace near the water. Every detail in Kelp Wanted, from the sage-green walls to the handmade ceramic mugs, was chosen to reflect a sense of slow and mindful living. We wanted to create a space that feels like a deep breath, warm, grounded, and quietly joyful.

When you stay here, you’re a short walk from the beach, the forest trails, and Seabrook’s small cafés and shops. Whether you’re sipping coffee on the porch, reading by the fire, or exploring tide pools, we hope you feel at home in the rhythm of the coast.

🌿 Kelp Wanted is our gentle reminder to stay connected to nature, the sea, the wind, and the green calm of the world around us. Thank you for being part of our story.
